Slide 1 · A safer ride

Alexander Miles patented the automatic elevator door on October 11, 1887.

His invention helped make elevator travel safer by improving the way elevator doors opened and closed.

Slide 2 · Early years

Alexander Miles was born on May 18, 1838, in Pickaway County, Ohio.

He later moved to Waukesha, Wisconsin, where he earned a living as a barber.

Slide 3 · A new family

After moving to Winona, Minnesota, Alexander met and married Mrs. Candace J. Dunlap of La Porte, Indiana.

She was a widow with two children.

Slide 4 · Grace

Together, Alexander Miles and Mrs. Candace J. Dunlap had a daughter named Grace.

Grace was born in 1876.

Slide 5 · A story of safety

Some people believed Alexander got the idea for his elevator-door mechanism after Grace accidentally fell down a shaft, almost ending her life.

Whether or not the story is true, his invention focused attention on making elevator travel safer.

Slide 6 · A lasting legacy

Alexander Miles died in 1918 and was inducted into the National Inventors Hall of Fame in 2007.

His work is remembered as an important contribution to elevator safety and invention.

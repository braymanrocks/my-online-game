Africa Conquest

A two-player, Risk-style battle for 30 African territories.

Blue player's turn

Deploy your reinforcements by selecting one of your territories.

Phase: Deploy
Reinforcements: 0
Blue territories: 0
Red territories: 0

How to play: Click one of your territories, then an adjacent enemy to select an attack. During fortify, select two adjacent territories you own to move one army. The attacker needs at least two armies.
